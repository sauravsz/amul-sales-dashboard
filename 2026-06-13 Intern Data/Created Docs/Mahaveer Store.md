Market Observation Point:

1. Amul Taaza and Amul Gold were the most visible products across the majority of retail outlets visited today. Their strong shelf presence and regular availability indicate consistent customer demand, making them the primary drivers of dairy product sales in this beat.
2. Amul products such as Amulya, Amulspray, Butter, and Mithai Mate were prominently visible in most retail outlets visited today. Their widespread availability suggests steady market demand, regular stock replenishment, and strong retailer confidence in these value-added dairy products.
3. Amul Ghee products were observed in comparatively fewer outlets than other dairy categories. Limited shelf presence may indicate lower customer demand, higher product pricing, slower stock movement, or retailers prioritizing faster-moving dairy products within available shelf space.
4. Demand for Amul Fresh Cream and Cheese was observed in one or two selected outlets during the market visit. Although demand remains limited, these products are showing potential in stores catering to customers with specific cooking and bakery requirements.
5. Amul Tin Paneer was observed in only one outlet during the market visit, indicating relatively low demand for the product. Retailers mentioned that the higher purchase price of approximately ₹110 per piece makes it less competitive compared to alternative paneer brands such as Ajanta, Prayag, and Red Cow, which are available in the ₹90–100 price range and therefore experience better market acceptance.
6. Amul beverage products such as Amul Tru, Lassi, and Amul Kool were visible in several retail outlets during the market visit. Their presence indicates steady consumer demand for ready-to-drink dairy beverages, particularly among customers seeking convenient and refreshing beverage options.
7. One retailer reported receiving damaged packs of Amul Tea Special Rusk during delivery. The damaged condition of the product affected its saleability and retailer satisfaction, highlighting the need for improved handling and transportation practices to minimize product damage.
