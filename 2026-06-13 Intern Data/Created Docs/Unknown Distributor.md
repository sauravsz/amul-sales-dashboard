Edible Oil Category – Market Pricing, Retail Insights & Competitive Landscape

1.The edible oil category remains highly competitive, with multiple established brands such as Engine, Mahakosh, Tiger, Fortune, Mast Oil, King's, and Sreeji competing for market share across the visited outlets. Retailers reported that customer purchase decisions are primarily influenced by price, brand familiarity, and product availability.

2.Retailer interactions revealed that market selling prices for edible oils often differ from the printed MRP due to competitive pricing practices. For example, Engine Oil (1 litre) was reported to have a PTR of approximately ₹190–195 and an MRP of ₹210, while retailers typically sell it at around ₹205 to remain competitive in the market.

3.Retailers reported that Engine Oil (500 ml) is procured at approximately ₹64 against an MRP of ₹70, indicating a limited but steady margin structure within the category. Similar pricing patterns were observed across other leading edible oil brands.

4.Mahakosh Oil was reported to have a PTR ranging from ₹130–134 against an MRP of ₹138, while Fortune Oil was available with a PTR of approximately ₹146 against an MRP of ₹150. Retailers indicated that these brands maintain stable demand due to their established market presence and customer trust.

5.Tiger Oil continues to attract price-sensitive consumers due to its economical pricing. Retailers reported that the 1-litre pack has an MRP of ₹120 with a PTR of approximately ₹115, contributing to regular sales movement in the lower-price segment of the edible oil market.

Distribution Network Assessment & Retailer Outreach

1.Retailers in the Katigorah market area reported sourcing edible oil products primarily from distributors operating in Silchar and Badarpur. This indicates the market's dependence on nearby distribution networks for product supply and replenishment.
