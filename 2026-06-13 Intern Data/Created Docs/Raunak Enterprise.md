Observations:

During the market visit, it was observed that due to lower temperatures and continuous rainfall, many areas were facing electricity issues. Because of this, several shop’s refrigerators and freezers were not functioning properly, which directly affected the storage and sales of ice cream products. Retailers were therefore placing comparatively lower orders for ice cream. 

In one of the shops visited, the Amul refrigerator itself was not working, due to which the shop owner did not place any order for ice cream products.

Many retailers mentioned that ₹10 ice cream products still had strong customer demand. However, due to limited supply availability of ₹10 SKUs, the retailer demand could not be fulfilled properly.

Along with ice cream products, most of the shops showed good demand for dairy products from the dairy section. Retailers also reported demand for frozen snack items such as pizza and French fries, indicating growing customer interest in frozen food categories beyond ice cream.
