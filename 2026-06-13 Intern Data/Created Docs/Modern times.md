Observations
1. Despite active pitching, most retailers showed limited interest in stocking Amul beverages. Amul Tru and Kool have slow movement in this locality, with products like Thumbs Up, Paperboat, Maaza, Lahori Jeera, and Sting dominating shelf space across outlets.
2. Non Stop brand drinks (litchi, mango, etc. in 140ml packs) have significant fridge penetration in this beat, leaving little room for Amul Tru. Their PTR stands at ₹6.50 per piece (₹260/case of 40 pcs) against an MRP of ₹10 — a margin retailers find difficult to pass up, regardless of Amul Tru's taste, ingredient quality and brand value.
3. Mother's Dairy flavoured milk and lassi were observed at several outlets. Trade pricing noted at Lokenath Store — Flavoured Milk: PTR ₹24.50 / MRP ₹30; Lassi: PTR ₹17.50 / MRP ₹20. MilkyMist 200g frozen paneer was also present at the same outlet — PTR ₹85 / MRP ₹120.
4. The SD Amul Parlour reported unexpectedly strong sales of pedas and butter cake. The outlet wished to reorder but stock was unavailable at the distributor's end.
5. Demand for cow milk (3 outlets) and 100g cheese slices (4 outlets) was noted across the beat, but could not be fulfilled due to stock unavailability at the distributor level.
