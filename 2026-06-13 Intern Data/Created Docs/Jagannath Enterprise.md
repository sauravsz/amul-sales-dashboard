*400g:* Retails at ₹110 (PTR: ₹90.00). This translates to ₹275.00 per kg, offering the retailer an 18.2% margin.
*1kg:* Retails at ₹180 (PTR: ₹167.00). This translates to a highly aggressive ₹180.00 per kg, offering the retailer a razor-thin 7.2% margin.

*Takeaways*
Where most retailers are acquiring similar variant products from local wholesaler at a slightly inflated price than Agency, it still cost them less than Amul. This profit margin gap discourages retailers to sell amul malai paneer. 
Amul offers the highest consumer price in the market (up to ₹376 per kg in case of 200g pack) but backs this up by offering the highest channel margins (peaking at 21.7% for the 425g SKU) to ensure retailers actively stock and promote their product.

*Takeaways*
Amul has priced its Rusk exactly the same as Ruskit for the end consumer. However, Amul’s lower PTR gives the retailer an extra 2.6% margin. In fast-moving consumer goods, this slight margin advantage at identical consumer price points is a textbook strategy to win retailer preference.
