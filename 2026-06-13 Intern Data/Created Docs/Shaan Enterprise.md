Market observations

Most retailers reported a significant decline in sales due to the ongoing summer vacation at NIT Silchar. Since students are not attending the campus regularly, footfall in nearby stores has reduced considerably, resulting in lower sales.

Despite the slowdown, there is good scope for chocolates, sweets, organic items and cookies in stores located near the NIT campus.

Among sweets, Kaju Katli and Lal Peda were found to have strong demand.

In the cookies category, T Special Rusk, Butter Toast, Almond Nankhatai, and Cashew Nankhatai showed higher demand.

Among chocolates, Fruit Nut Chocolate both 40g and 150g bars emerged as a high-demand product, along with Amul Velvet Chocolate. Sugar-Free Dark Chocolate and Dark Chocolate both 75g and 150g bars continue to have moderate to good demand.

In the beverages segment, Amul Kool Café (both plastic and glass bottle variants) recorded high demand. Amul Kool Café Can showed moderate demand, while Amul Kool Badam registered strong demand in this beat.

In the organic product category, Organic Atta and Organic Sugar were in high demand across most outlets.

Amul Lassi (150 ml pack) also demonstrated strong demand in the market.

Out of the 15 outlets visited, orders were placed at 6 outlets only. The lower ordering trend indicates a significant reduction in retailers' replenishment requirements, primarily due to decreased sales during the NIT summer vacation period. As overall sales have declined, retailers have reduced their order quantities and ordering frequency accordingly.

Several retailers expressed demand for products such as Bindass Chocolate, Amul Butter (₹35 pack), Amul Masti Dahi, and Lal Peda. However, orders for these items could not be placed as they were unavailable due to stock shortages. The unavailability of these products resulted in missed sales opportunities in the visited outlets.
